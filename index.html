ABOUT CANDACE

Clarity before you take the next step.

Candace Pvt. Ltd. is envisioned as a student-first overseas education organization helping learners make informed decisions about international study.

We focus on transparent counseling, realistic university shortlists and structured support throughout the application journey.

Everything you need for your study-abroad journey.
